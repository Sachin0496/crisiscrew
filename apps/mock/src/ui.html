From customer tickets to a reviewed fix, with the on-call engineer still on the phone.

Connecting Console ↗

24 customers write in. Watch CrisisCrew take it from tickets to a reviewed fix.

It groups the tickets, pages the on-call engineer and briefs her by phone. While she's still on the call, it clones the repo, reproduces the bug, fixes it with OpenCode, and sends her the report and a pull request. She reviews the fix. She doesn't write it.
